# Wardrobe Check

Use only in rest time, when 帅东 wants to work on 穿搭. Never let it eat study time.

Rules:
- Start with his closet only: what he already owns, what fits, what he actually wears. No shopping list yet.
- Do not build a full outfit until he gives: payday date, whole-outfit budget, height and weight, and preferred style. He said the full outfit waits until payday.
- Hard budget line: total monthly spending ≤ 2000 yuan. Any single item must be checked against that line, and non-essential spending gets a warning first.
- No purchase, no order, no payment without his explicit approval for that item.
